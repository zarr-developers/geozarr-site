# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 6 October 2026

- **Date:** Tuesday 6 October 2026, 9:00 AM US Eastern
- **Attendees:** Max Jones (chair), Eugene Yu, Patrick Van Laake, Emmanuel Mathot, Konstantin Ntokas
- **Agenda:** Round-robin updates, Q&A, implications of CF developments, xarray coders proposal, V1 and charter timeline

## 1. Updates

- **CF community workshop (Bonn, September).** Presentations on GeoZarr and on encoding CF data in GeoZarr drew many questions. A breakout session on separating CF semantics from the NetCDF encoding was the best attended of the week and ended with general agreement to pursue it, with a proposal targeted for next year's workshop. The discussion continues in [cf-convention discussion 484](https://github.com/orgs/cf-convention/discussions/484). Workshop notes will be published on the CF conventions website.
- **Cloud-Native Geospatial Forum.** GeoZarr's status is being presented at the forum this week.
- **EOPF.** `zarr-cm` has been updated for the v0.1 convention releases, and a GeoZarr driver has been developed for the EOPF processing framework, so that Sentinel-1, -2 and -3 outputs follow the GeoZarr conventions (with caveats for Sentinel-1).

## 2. Questions

- **Is there a validator?** Yes, [geozarr-toolkit](https://github.com/zarr-developers/geozarr-toolkit#validation). It needs a small update for the latest convention releases.
- **Are there EOPF examples?** Yes, in the [EOPF Explorer catalogue](https://api.explorer.eopf.copernicus.eu/browser/stac/collections/sentinel-2-l2a-new).

## 3. Discussion

- **CF timing.** GeoZarr will not wait for the CF process. The working assumption is the same split of semantics from encoding. Coordinates are within GeoZarr's scope; constructs unrelated to geolocation, such as ancillary variables, external variables and flags, would be regular Zarr conventions outside GeoZarr.
- **xarray coders proposal.** [xarray pull request 11541](https://github.com/pydata/xarray/pull/11541) proposes moving CF encoding and decoding out of the xarray core into a layer of coders that backends can choose between. This would let a custom backend handle GeoZarr-specific decoding. An example backend with out-of-group references and several GeoZarr conventions is [xarray-zarr-xgroup](https://github.com/pvanlaake/xarray-zarr-xgroup), discussed in [xarray discussion 11609](https://github.com/pydata/xarray/discussions/11609).

## 4. V1 and charter timeline

- Proposal: proceed with a V1 release of the three conventions already in use (`proj`, `spatial`, `multiscales`), with explicit coordinates following in a later version. No one present objected. A concern raised earlier, that a V1 without coordinates could delay work on them, is noted.
- The V1 specification document will reference the conventions and not duplicate them.
- The SWG voting roster was lost in the move to Agora and has to be restored with OGC staff before a vote.
- Plan: update the draft charter and send another call for comments, then hold the SWG vote on the charter. The November meeting covers how the three conventions are put to the OGC Technical Committee, with an SWG vote on V1 targeted for the December meeting.
