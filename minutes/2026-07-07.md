# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 7 July 2026

## Meeting Purpose

Review the revised SWG charter draft

## Key Takeaways

- The community is invited to comment on the draft revised charter
- Interested parties are encouraged to review the two-pager on OGC Measures
- Felix Cremer is working on a Julia implementation based on multiscales

## Topics

### Challenges with keeping track of materials

- Kathi raised the challenges of finding materials for GeoZarr, given differences between GeoZarr and other SWGs.
    - Max will reach out to Scott to let the TC know that some coordination across SWGs would help

### Revised SWG charter

- Max presented the draft recharter, explaining the draft should reflect the direction embarked upon since the pivot initiated by Christophe in December 2024
    - Community members are invited to comment on the revised charter, through GitHub discussions and/or Agora

### Discussion on multiscales

- Felix Cremer is working on a Julia implementation and has questions about how the transform property relates between spatial and multiscales, which were answered by Emmanuel Mathot.

### Feedback on spatial / coords

- Felix also raised confusion about why spatial is segregated from a coordinates convention, which Emmanuel also responded to.

### Discussion on community process

- Patrick Van Laake raised concerns about how the GeoZarr re-charter was drafted. He wanted clarification about who drafted the draft, and expressed dissatisfaction that the revised charter was drafted by an individual rather than multiple people. He asked for it to be noted in the minutes that he doesn't find the process to be sufficiently community oriented. Patrick expressed surprise that CF was mentioned throughout the re-charter but the CS convention was not. Max explained that the draft is a first version, developed in a limited amount of time, and was opened as a pull request on GitHub specifically to have a community engagement period. Max also clarified that while the initial draft was independently authored, it was informed by minutes on past OGC SWG meetings and GitHub discussions. Max committed to sharing materials and agendas for future meetings further in advance of future meetings, and suggested soliciting input from others on how to best moderate a SWG that is committed to upstream community engagement.
