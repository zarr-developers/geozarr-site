# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 7 July 2026

**Date:** Tuesday 7 July 2026, 9:00 AM US Eastern
**Participants named in the minutes:** Max Jones (chair), Kathi Schleidt, Felix Cremer, Emmanuel Mathot, Patrick Van Laake
**Subject of the meeting:** Review the revised SWG charter draft

*Restructured in September 2026. These minutes were drafted by hand (no transcript); content unchanged apart from a corrected pivot date.*

## 1. Tracking materials across SWGs

Kathi raised the challenge of finding materials for GeoZarr, given the differences between GeoZarr and other SWGs. Max will reach out to Scott to let the TC know that some coordination across SWGs would help.

## 2. Revised SWG charter

Max presented the draft recharter, explaining that it should reflect the direction embarked upon since the pivot initiated by Christophe in December 2025. Community members are invited to comment on the revised charter through GitHub discussions and/or Agora.

## 3. Multiscales: Julia implementation

Felix Cremer is working on a Julia implementation based on multiscales and had questions about how the transform property relates between spatial and multiscales, which Emmanuel Mathot answered.

## 4. Feedback on spatial and coords

Felix also raised confusion about why spatial is segregated from a coordinates convention, to which Emmanuel also responded.

## 5. Community process

Patrick Van Laake raised concerns about how the GeoZarr recharter was drafted. He wanted clarification about who drafted it, and expressed dissatisfaction that the revised charter was drafted by an individual rather than multiple people. He asked for it to be noted in the minutes that he does not find the process to be sufficiently community oriented. Patrick expressed surprise that CF was mentioned throughout the recharter but the CS convention was not. Max explained that the draft is a first version, developed in a limited amount of time, and was opened as a pull request on GitHub specifically to have a community engagement period; while the initial draft was independently authored, it was informed by minutes of past OGC SWG meetings and GitHub discussions. Max committed to sharing materials and agendas further in advance of future meetings, and suggested soliciting input from others on how best to moderate a SWG that is committed to upstream community engagement.
