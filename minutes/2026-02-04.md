# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 4 February 2026

**Date:** Wednesday 4 February 2026
**Attendees named in the transcript:** Max Jones (chair), Christophe Noël, Kathi Schleidt, Patrick Van Laake, Chris Little, Guilherme Castelão, Emmanuel Mathot, Wietze Suijker, Len Strnad, Tom Nicholas, Atanas Balevsky
**Subject of the meeting:** Chair handover, strawman roadmap to V1, CF alignment, convention adoption

*Revised in September 2026 against the meeting transcript.*

## 1. Chair handover

Christophe, as outgoing chair, formally stated that Max Jones is now officially chair. A co-chair is sought; OGC membership is required, since the role includes voting eligibility for discussions that do not reach consensus (this ruled out Wietze's earlier offer). No volunteers came forward; requests can go to Max at any time.

## 2. New participant: Kathi Schleidt

Kathi introduced herself: chair of the OGC Observations, Measurements and Samples (OMS) group and a co-chair on TimeseriesML, which is examining encodings for time-series data, Zarr among them. Her aim, achieved previously with CoverageJSON, is to bring observational semantics into gridded and cube formats: not just which property a value represents, but how it was measured and by what methodology. She gave the group fair warning that this is what she will pursue. She also flagged onboarding friction: the working repository (geozarr-spec under the zarr-developers organization) is found via Agora, unlike CovJSON, which has moved to an OGC repository.

## 4. Strawman: the path to V1

Max proposed organizing the remaining work into three milestones: a V1 Release Candidate (all constituent conventions at candidate stability, meaning mostly stable with breaking changes requiring new versions), a V1 Architecture Board review, and a V1 SWG vote, targeting presentation to the OGC architecture board in the summer. Fifteen proposed issues, currently Markdown in a branch, will become GitHub issues on a project board that Max will create and circulate by email within the week. Per the strawman convention Chris recommended, the proposal stands unless someone presents a better alternative; concerns about milestones or target dates are due before the March meeting, after which silence counts as approval.

From the discussion: Chris advised drafting in Markdown or AsciiDoc and involving OGC staff for the Metanorma conversion only at the end. Patrick asked how the earlier "strategic reset" items map onto this and argued that GeoZarr is missing an explicit concept of a coordinate system, ideally hooked to OGC referencing by coordinates; Max pointed to the Proj convention as the CRS registration mechanism and asked Patrick to review it and open a concrete issue with example metadata (Patrick later clarified his concern sits with the Spatial convention rather than Proj). Chris asked that "spatial" always be read as spatio-temporal. Kathi asked where cell-content semantics would hook in; the "guidance for new components of GeoZarr" item will carry it, with total backwards compatibility plus enrichment as the goal, as was done for CovJSON.

## 5. Convention adoption updates

- **EOPF Explorer V1 launched.** Emmanuel launched EOPF Explorer V1 that week: to his knowledge the first adoption of the Spatial, Proj, and Multiscales Zarr conventions for Sentinel data, with a page tracing the GeoZarr data-model history and a technical deep-dive page listing open issues. OpenLayers and TiTiler support it; feedback goes via the project's Discourse. Known limitation: data are encoded as float32 because Zarr v3 does not yet support fixed scale/offset, hurting performance until integer re-encoding becomes possible.
- **rioxarray.** The first PR of Emmanuel's series was accepted by Alan Snow, creating a framework for conventions in rioxarray; Spatial and Proj PRs come next, after which rioxarray should open Zarr-conventions data.
- **Chunk manifest (Len Strnad, Wherobots).** Len described large-scale Zarr-in/Zarr-out pipelines adopting Spatial and Proj and using Multiscales, and pitched a chunk-manifest idea: a sidecar GeoParquet indexing the non-empty chunks in space, so sparse global mosaics need not probe empty ocean chunks. Max and Tom Nicholas concluded it is an implementation-level optimization below the spec (how cheaply a reader learns whether a chunk is initialized does not change Zarr conformance), so not a GeoZarr matter; Len will share the repository link, and the ESIP cloud computing cluster work on standardizing chunk manifests (Aimee Barciauskas, James Gallagher) is the natural venue.

## 6. Convention maturity criteria

Borrowing from the STAC ecosystem: a convention moves from proposal to candidate once three implementations are documented, and from candidate to stable at the V1 vote (after which changes require a full review process and new release). Nobody disagreed. Emmanuel will keep a status issue on where Spatial, Multiscales, and GeoProj stand against this bar and propose promotions at a subsequent meeting; he expects more implementations to appear once formal releases exist.

Max will start an FAQ in geozarr-spec (tagging Kathi and other newcomers) and, per Kathi's request, turn the Agora page into a proper landing page linking all the relevant repositories, since the material now spans several organizations.
