# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 4 March 2026

**Date:** Wednesday 4 March 2026
**Attendees:** Max Jones (chair, Development Seed), Tyler Erickson (VorGeo, Radiant Earth), Patrick Van Laake, Guilherme Castelão (NREL), Max Guntrum (DWD, first time), Colby Fisher, Ethan Davis, Emmanuel Mathot, Matthias Mohr, Wietze Suijker
**Subject of the meeting:** February follow-ups, CF alignment, implementation readiness, OGC governance

*Revised in September 2026 against the meeting transcript.*

## 1. Welcome

Max Guntrum introduced himself: at the German meteorological service (DWD) since mid-2025, with a background in remote sensing and imaging radar, working in a cross-functional team modernizing internal storage systems and customer-facing data APIs; the team's interest is the intersection of Zarr/GeoZarr and the CF convention. DWD rejoined OGC about a month earlier. The meeting was shortened slightly: Max Jones left five minutes early to host a Pangeo Showcase at which Wietze presented GeoZarr rendering in QGIS powered by GDAL (a public, recorded Zoom).

## 2. Chair updates

- The roadmap agreed in February now exists as issues and a project board in geozarr-spec; "to do" issues are entry points for new contributors.
- A website was created as a landing page for newcomers, linking the conventions, the spec repo, and the Agora page, with V1 progress synced from the project board for people who dislike GitHub project boards. (Christophe had commented by email that it might not be necessary; Max invited others' views.)
- An online validator now takes the URL of a publicly accessible Zarr store, validates it, and lets the user inspect array and group metadata, with a Sentinel-2 example Max converted from TIFF. It is built on geozarr-toolkit, a Python validator for the Zarr conventions that also helps produce conformant metadata.

## 3. CF alignment

Patrick's spun-out discussion on the CF GitHub is by far the most trending there, with two branches: maintaining CF backwards compatibility and moving existing NetCDF constructs into Zarr, versus looking at Zarr and CF afresh from a Zarr perspective, which Patrick champions. He reported a joint view emerging that there is a value proposition for CF branching out beyond NetCDF. Ethan added that the CF governance panel meets on 19 March with this on the agenda; the open structural question is where a non-backwards-compatible CF-on-Zarr would reside (CF 2.0? CF-Zarr?). Dave Blodgett is writing up a Zarr-extension-focused proposal.

**Decided:** the SWG waits while the CF community hashes out its internal strategy, engaging when Dave Blodgett's material lands. Max will post the link in the geozarr-spec CF-alignment issue, tag Ethan to confirm it is the right one, and follow up between meetings.

Separately, Patrick reported his CRS-guidance action is effectively on hold, subsumed by his CS sample-convention work, which surfaced that sharing dimensions across arrays affects how CRSs are specified, aligning naturally with OGC's compound-CRS construction (X/Y, Z, and T defined separately and composed); it will still use the Proj convention.

## 4. Implementations and V1 readiness

Implementation matrix status: GDAL done pending release; rioxarray GeoProj merged but unreleased; OpenLayers read-only with fixed-scale-offset caveats; TiTiler fine; EOPF documentation still references GeoZarr 0.4 and needs updating to the Zarr conventions. A notes/disclaimer mechanism for the matrix was agreed, with an issue assigned to Emmanuel.

Max raised an independent-verification concern: the three-implementations criterion is technically met, but the implementations are all driven by one project (EOPF), leaving no independent check. Emmanuel agreed and proposed additionally requiring two or three distinct datasets, ideally including a CF or climate one. Ethan probed what counts as an implementation at all, noting OGC's counting rules say little about depth, coverage (read versus write), or real usage. Max will open a PR defining implementation criteria, looking at prior art from communities such as Apache Arrow; Tyler suggested ordering matrix entries by adoption so the significant implementations stand out.

The two most pressing V1 items: defining the minimum requirements for a dataset to qualify as GeoZarr (which of the conventions must be present), and, following validator feedback, validating all conventions in one pass and reporting standing on each rather than a single pass/fail (Tyler's suggestion; Max will implement).

Gaps in the matrix: R (Patrick will email details of his CF-on-NetCDF R library) and Julia (Patrick will reach out to his contacts working with Zarr and NetCDF in Julia).

## 5. OGC governance and hosting

- **Voting membership.** The Agora agreement page (typing "I agree", then a 30-day wait) is effectively the SWG member list, restricted to OGC members; Ethan cautioned an upgrade or request step may also be needed under the new system. Max will clarify the mechanics with OGC staff (Scott Simmons and Greg) and report back.
- **IP clause.** OGC's technical committee policies require a statement that accepted contributions shall be owned by OGC, which the group has not been carrying. Tyler found the clause startling; Ethan explained GeoZarr chose to be a regular OGC standard rather than a community standard precisely to lean on OGC's governance (CovJSON, by contrast, is a community standard managed within OGC), and that he navigates the rules by asking Scott and Greg. Nothing will merge on this before the next meeting; Max will meet OGC staff first and also have an offline chat with Ethan about SWG chairing. The OGC TC meeting in Philadelphia was that same week.
- **HackMD.** Meeting notes moved off Brianna's personal account (which was hitting free-tier limits) to a GeoZarr team; Tyler volunteered as the second admin to reduce the bus factor.
- **GitHub hosting.** An open issue asks whether GeoZarr materials stay under zarr-developers/zarr-conventions or move to a branded GeoZarr organization; 30 seconds of everyone's thought requested.
