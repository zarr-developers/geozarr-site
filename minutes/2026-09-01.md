# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 1 September 2026

- **Date:** Tuesday 1 September 2026, 9:00 AM US Eastern
- **Attendees:** Max Jones (chair), Eugene Yu, Emmanuel Mathot
- **Agenda:** Member updates, findings from the explicit-coordinates working sessions, path to a first release, recharter

## 1. Meeting time

Turnout was small. The current slot (9 AM Eastern, 3 PM central Europe, 6 AM Pacific) stays for now, with another scheduling survey to be considered after US daylight saving ends.

## 2. Member updates

- Interest from a data provider with largely NetCDF holdings in cloud-optimized formats that can be read directly, without a service between the data and clients. Pointers given: the [implementations page](/implementations) and the virtual Zarr co-working series now hosted by ESIP.
- GeoHEIF/GIMI was raised as prior art for compact CRS identifiers (a short code whose full definition is retrieved externally).
- A decision framework for choosing a cloud-native format, including GeoZarr, is open as a pull request to the cloud-native geospatial guide, alongside an open benchmarking framework for format comparisons. Both came out of a study for CNES.
- GeoZarr will be presented at the Cloud-Native Geospatial Forum in October.
- ESA will continue EOPF work in the coming year, including Zarr-Python improvements and an async GeoZarr reader for TiTiler.

## 3. Explicit coordinates: working-session findings

- Explicit 1D coordinates are the agreed must-have.
- Most participants also want 2D coordinate arrays, cell bounds, non-standard calendars, band names or category labels, and coordinates shared within a group.
- Parametric vertical coordinates and DGGS were minority needs.
- The one sharp disagreement is whether GeoZarr should recommend exactly one way to do each thing.
- A written summary will be circulated.

## 4. Release strategy

- Proposal discussed: release 1.0 with the current three conventions and develop explicit coordinates in parallel as a non-breaking 1.1. Those present supported it; the chair will follow up with members who want coordinates in version 1.
- Two proposals for explicit coordinates exist (`coords` and `CS`). No choice between them was made. Consensus is preferred; a vote of the SWG's voting members is the fallback.
- The SWG voting roster was lost in the move to Agora and is being restored.
- A small in-person working session in Europe on the coordinates proposals will be explored.

## 5. Recharter

No comments have been received on the recharter since its last update. A formal SWG vote will be called.
