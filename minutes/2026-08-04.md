# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 4 August 2026

- **Date:** Tuesday 4 August 2026, 9:00 AM US Eastern
- **Attendees:** Max Jones (chair), Eugene Yu, Kathi Schleidt, Patrick Van Laake, Wietze Suijker, Emmanuel Mathot
- **Agenda:** Recap, announcements, Q&A for newcomers, review of the revised SWG charter

## 1. Recap

The chair recapped the December 2025 move to a profile of small, composable Zarr conventions, the V1 deliverable of `proj`, `spatial` and `multiscales`, and the rechartering exercise that followed from concerns about explicit coordinates.

Correction requested for the record: the concern about a V1 without explicit coordinates was raised by Christophe Noël, not Patrick Van Laake. Patrick agrees that explicit coordinates are needed but does not consider that they must be part of V1.

## 2. Announcements

- The agenda and running notes have moved from HackMD to a Google Doc.
- A Google Calendar for SWG meetings is available to subscribe to.
- Three working sessions on explicit coordinates are scheduled. They cover the same topics, so attending one is enough. Notes from side meetings are kept in separate documents linked from the main notes document.
- rioxarray 0.23 was released with GeoZarr write support.

## 3. Revised charter

The group went through the comments on the charter pull request.

- The charter is reviewed in Markdown. The HTML will be generated from AsciiDoc afterwards with no substantive changes.
- The Intended Capabilities table stays in the charter as a statement of scope. The capabilities targeted for V1 will be listed under Initial Deliverables, not marked in the table.
- Section 3.1 will be reduced to prose and the prior-art details moved into a column of the table, with a statement that prior art is not binding.
- A "General" subheading will be added under Scope of Work so that the introductory text can be referenced by clause.
- The "standard name" wording will be restated as describing the observed property.
- OMS will be added as related art, and any OMS capabilities not already covered will be added to the table. Whether observational semantics should be a separate, composable convention or needs a hook in the GeoZarr model was discussed without agreement and goes to a follow-up call.

The aim is a stable charter text within a couple of weeks, followed by an open comment period. Comments are welcome on the GitHub pull request or by email to the chair.
