# GeoZarr SWG Monthly Meeting, 6 May 2026

**Date:** Wednesday 6 May 2026 (run an hour earlier than announced: the invite said 3 PM UTC instead of 4 PM after the daylight-saving change, Max's error)
**Attendees named in the transcript:** Max Jones (chair), Patrick Van Laake, Konstantin Ntokas, Tyler Erickson, Lucy Seabourne (first time), Colby Fisher, Lazaro Alonso (arrived at the announced time)

*Revised in September 2026 against the meeting transcript.*

## 1. Convention governance and maintenance

Patrick raised four governance concerns: the process for getting a convention approved needs fleshing out; long-term persistence is unaddressed (archives can outlive a GitHub organization by a decade or more); versioning needs a mechanism for chains of updates and for signaling breaking changes; and identification is flawed, since the schema requires only one of UUID, schema URL, or spec URL, and a spec URL is not machine-readable.

Max scoped the discussion: the Zarr Conventions spec is cross-discipline (it supersedes ZEP0004, was developed with the Zarr steering council at the Zarr Summit, and Alistair Miles's explanatory blog post remains unpublished), while the GeoZarr conventions are the blessed subset to be submitted through OGC.

**Decided:**
- The UUID becomes the required identifier, with schema and spec URLs optional extras (all GeoZarr conventions already define UUIDs, whose load-bearing role is under-documented); Patrick is encouraged to open the correcting PRs.
- Max will get the Zarr Steering Council on a call before the next meeting, with Patrick invited, covering the framework concerns and the placement question below.
- On persistence, Max favors a distributed model: Zenodo for chartered permanence plus fast HTTP copies (for example on S3); thoughts go to the hosting-location issue.
- The process for admitting a convention into GeoZarr will be documented: consensus-driven via issues in geozarr-spec discussed at these meetings. Patrick suggested minimum requirements plus a no-objection staging month; Max will draft inclusion criteria and ping the group, noting a blessed convention need not itself be spatial (a time convention, say) but must be composable.

Max also asked for review of the convention-maturity PR and of the versioning strawman (issue 102), where Julia Signell has commented from the STAC perspective, an approach quite different from CF's; both need decisions before the OGC submission.

Patrick also proposed moving the conventions registration out of Zarr's `attributes` (user-defined per the core spec, hence collision-prone) into a top-level `conventions` metadata item. Max explained the history: a new top-level key requires a ZEP, and ZEP0004 has stalled repeatedly since 2023; the attributes placement is technically less ideal but achievable, and the non-nested layout lets existing practice (standard names, say) be registered backwards-compatibly, with validators enforcing consistency. The steering council call is the venue to test whether a better path exists, and it should happen before V1.

## 2. Proj toward V1

Context for the burst of issues: Christophe reviewed several conventions, and Kyle Barron implemented GeoZarr support in deck.gl-raster (browser rendering) and flagged what he sees as suboptimal in Proj, Spatial, and Multiscales. Emmanuel is pressing to release Proj V1 per the roadmap. Items resolved or teed up:

- **Repository rename.** GeoProj becomes Proj, matching the convention's name. No objections.
- **Colon removal.** The trailing colon comes off convention names (`proj:` becomes `proj`); attribute prefixes such as `proj:code` stay. Konstantin noted colon namespacing is common in STAC but understood the critique; the same change applies to Spatial and to the naming recommendation in the Zarr Conventions spec itself. A PR will stay open a few days for comment.
- **Multiple CRS representations.** Emmanuel proposes recommending an authoritative representation (WKT2) when several are present. Max's stated view: keeping them consistent is the validator's job, and making *a* decision matters more than which; the proposal merges in about a week absent objections.
- **OGC terminology.** Patrick finds the Proj text inconsistent with OGC terminology ("projection authority" is not a term). To avoid delaying the release, Max offered to point Claude at the spec and the OGC documentation and open a PR for Patrick to review.
- **Transform order.** Felix Cremer asked for GDAL's ordering; Kyle documented that the matrix-algebra (top-left) ordering is shared with STAC and the Rust GeoTIFF library. Resolved with an FAQ entry explaining the rationale; no objections.

## 3. Spatial and the Sentinel-1 gap

The three-dimensional language in Spatial must be either removed or properly specified; the Patrick/Kyle thread awaits Max's input. Konstantin (ESA EOPF) raised a larger gap: how should GeoZarr describe Sentinel-1 Level-1 products, whose axes are zero-Doppler azimuth time and slant range with no conventional CRS? Radar products are currently uncovered, and his extension project starting in August is expected to be GeoZarr-compliant. Patrick responded that his CS convention, implementing the OGC referencing-by-coordinates standard using CF constructs for any number of dimensions and any axis type, should serve this; an initial version is online and an improved one lands within two weeks.

**Decided:** Patrick and Konstantin will discuss directly, and a CS-convention readout is the first agenda item for June. A call between Konstantin and Emmanuel on EOPF visualization was also suggested. (Side note from the exchange: Sentinel-1 ships as TIFF and Sentinel-3 as NetCDF.)

## 4. Multiscales

- **Ordering rejected.** Kyle's request that levels be ordered finest to coarsest was declined: ordering is ambiguous across projections and units and for non-square scales, and enforcing it burdens producers and validators for a small reader convenience (Tyler and Konstantin concurring).
- **Composition rules.** Kyle's proposed revision is more involved; the group will review asynchronously before the next meeting.
- **Scope.** Multiscales for vector data and tile-matrix-set alignment semantics are not required for the architecture board submission (Tyler agreeing).

## 5. GeoZarr and CF

Max, attending EGU that week, reported heavy Zarr usage and one pointed comment from a session convener: make sure GeoZarr is not abandoning CF. He sees a real risk that the modeling world falls behind Earth observation, which is implementation-driven through EOPF, and will prepare a response recommending the group codify what people already do, excepting the clearly problematic parts (such as `spatial_ref`).

Patrick's CF readout: the discussions are in a lull; two outcomes stand. The CF community is looking into Dave Blodgett's NZ convention (Dave being a CF conventions author), which UCAR may adopt alongside NCZarr, with the caution that NZ is NetCDF-in-Zarr rather than GeoZarr per se; and the community is taking the decoupling of CF semantics from the NetCDF format more seriously, a long-term effort. Jonathan Gregory's request to settle the GeoZarr/CF relationship by June will not be met ("that's just not gonna happen"). Patrick's CS convention, out within two weeks, could be included in the OGC submission if the group deems it worthwhile, and would let GeoZarr read roughly 90 percent of CF-compatible NetCDF data, leaving only the more esoteric constructs.

**Decided:** Max will prepare the response and pursue a CF-specific roadmap with buy-in from CF leadership, meaning the governance panel (chaired by Ethan Davis) and the CF committee (chaired by Jonathan Gregory).
